Our Master Chefs

Led by Chef Mohammed, our culinary team brings decades of expertise and passion for authentic Indian cuisine. Every dish is crafted with meticulous attention to detail, honoring traditional recipes while embracing innovation.

From the tandoor to your table, we ensure each meal is a masterpiece of flavour and presentation.

Traditional Methods,
Modern Excellence

We believe in the time-honoured traditions of Indian cooking. Our chefs use authentic techniques passed down through generations, from hand-grinding spices to slow-cooking curries to perfection.

Fresh ingredients, traditional methods, and genuine passion create the unforgettable dining experience that defines Chipstead Tandoori.

Tandoori Excellence

Our traditional clay oven, the tandoor, reaches temperatures of over 480°C, sealing in flavours and creating that distinctive char and smokiness that makes our tandoori dishes legendary.

From succulent chicken tikka to perfectly grilled lamb chops, each item is a masterpiece.

Balti Perfection

The Balti style of cooking brings together fresh ingredients, aromatic spices, and traditional techniques to create rich, flavourful curries that have become a British favourite.

Cooked and served in authentic Balti pans for maximum flavour.

Premium Cuts

We source only the finest cuts of meat, marinating them in our secret blend of spices for hours before grilling to perfection in our tandoor.

Each bite is tender, flavourful, and unforgettable.

Our Story

For over 25 years, Chipstead Tandoori has been serving the finest authentic Indian cuisine in Surrey. Our passion for traditional recipes, combined with the freshest ingredients, creates an unforgettable dining experience.

Every dish is crafted with love and attention to detail, honoring the rich culinary heritage of India while embracing modern culinary techniques.
